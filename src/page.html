Mobile

iOS and Android from one codebase

Cross-platform apps in Flutter, taken from empty repo through both store review queues.

  • Store submission, review responses, and release pipelines
  • In-app purchases, subscriptions, and paywalls
  • Offline-first data, local notifications, deep links
Web

Web applications and marketing sites

Interfaces that stay fast on a mid-range phone and still make sense at 4am when something breaks.

  • Product dashboards, portals, and internal tools
  • Static and server-rendered sites with real page-speed budgets
  • Payments, auth, and third-party integrations
AI

AI features that survive contact with users

Language-model features built with evaluation and cost tracking from the first commit, not bolted on after the demo.

  • Retrieval over your own documents and data

Rescue

Inherited codebases and stalled builds

An honest read on what you have, what it will cost to keep, and what should be rewritten — before anyone commits to a rebuild.

  • Test coverage and CI on projects that shipped without either
§ 02Process

How an engagement runs

Every project moves through the same five stages, in order. You can stop after any one of them.

01

Scoping call

Thirty minutes on what you are trying to ship and when. You leave with a written summary of the problem as we understood it, whether or not you hire us.

30 min · free
02

Discovery sprint

We map the build: user flows, data model, integrations, risks, and the parts we do not yet know enough about. Ends in a written scope and a fixed price for the build.

1–2 weeks · fixed fee
03

Build

Two-week increments against the agreed scope. You get a running build and a written change log at the end of each one, plus direct access to the engineers writing it.

Milestone billing
04

Launch and handover

Store submission or production deploy, then the repository, infrastructure, accounts, and documentation transfer to you. Nothing stays behind a login only we hold.

Included
05

Support, if you want it

A monthly retainer for maintenance, store policy changes, and the next set of features. Optional, cancellable, and never a condition of handover.

Monthly · optional

How we hold ourselves to it

Claims get checked. Anything we put in front of your users — a number, a date, a legal reference — is computed from real data or cited to a real source. If neither is possible, it does not ship.

You keep the keys. Repositories, cloud accounts, developer accounts, and domains are yours from day one, registered in your name.

No lock-in by omission. Documentation and deployment steps are part of the deliverable, not a follow-up quote.
