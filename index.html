How we work

Five stages, in order — and you can stop after any one of them

Every engagement runs the same way, so you always know what the next commitment is before you make it.

01

Scoping call

Thirty minutes on what you are trying to ship and when. You leave with a written summary of the problem as we understood it, whether or not you hire us.

30 minutes · free
02

Discovery sprint

We map the build: user flows, data model, integrations, risks, and the parts we do not yet know enough about. Ends in a written scope and a fixed price for the build.

1–2 weeks · fixed fee
03

Build

Two-week increments against the agreed scope. You get a running build and a written change log at the end of each one, plus direct access to the engineers writing it.

Milestone billing
04

Launch and handover

Store submission or production deploy, then the repository, infrastructure, accounts, and documentation transfer to you. Nothing stays behind a login only we hold.

Included
05

Support, if you want it

A monthly retainer for maintenance, store policy changes, and the next set of features. Optional, cancellable, and never a condition of handover.

Monthly · optional

How we hold ourselves to it

Three commitments we will put in the contract

Claims get checked

Anything we put in front of your users — a number, a date, a legal reference — is computed from real data or cited to a real source. If neither is possible, it does not ship.

You keep the keys

Repositories, cloud accounts, developer accounts, and domains are yours from day one, registered in your name — not ours.

No lock-in by omission

Documentation and deployment steps are part of the deliverable, not a follow-up quote. You can hand the project to another team without calling us.

Do you work with clients outside Canada?

Yes. We are incorporated in Canada and work remotely with clients worldwide. Contracts and invoicing are handled by Sofluent Technologies Inc.

Can you take over a project someone else started?

That is what the rescue engagement is for. We start with an assessment of the existing codebase and give you a written read on what to keep, what to replace, and what it will cost — before any rebuild is proposed.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. The repository, cloud accounts, and store developer accounts are registered in your name and stay with you at handover.

How do you price a build?

The discovery sprint is a fixed fee. It ends in a written scope and a fixed price for the build, billed against milestones. We do not quote a build price before discovery, because that number would be a guess.
